DO NOT RAISE: design preferences, naming, alternative approaches, or anything
about HOW to implement a task. Those are decided when the plan is built, and
raising them here costs the planner an interruption for nothing.

If you find nothing in those five classes, say so plainly. A clean review is
the common case and is worth more than a manufactured objection.

Write your review to a file, then hand it back:

    tandem-verdict --plan "{{PLAN}}" --role {{ROLE}} --review <file> --verdict clean|objections

Number each objection, name its class, and quote the exact plan text it is
about together with the exact evidence. For class 4, include the command you
ran and its output. The planner acts on this without reading your pane, so an
objection it cannot locate in the plan is one it cannot fix.

Pass --verdict objections if and only if you listed at least one objection.
Pass --verdict clean otherwise. Do not summarise the verdict in prose instead;
the flag is what is read.

You may ask about intent while reviewing:

    tandem-ask {{PLANNER}} "your question"
